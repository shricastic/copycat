Quiet until you need it

  • Search everything you copied

    Newest first, with how long ago you copied it. Start typing to narrow it down.

  • Pin what you reuse

    Pinned items stay at the top and are never cleared, even when the history fills up.

  • Passwords stay out

    Items your password manager marks as private are never recorded. Pause recording any time.

  • One shortcut, from anywhere

    ⌘⇧V opens Copycat next to your cursor. Change it to any keys you like.

  • Feels like part of your Mac

    Frosted glass, light and dark mode, your accent colour. No Dock icon, no window to manage.

  • Stays on your computer

    Your history is kept in one local file and is never sent anywhere.

Keyboard first

⌘⇧V Open or close Copycat, from any app
↑↓ Move through your history
↩ Copy the selected item
⌘P Pin or unpin it
⌘⌫ Delete it
⌘, Settings

On Windows, use Ctrl instead of ⌘.

Opening it the first time

Copycat isn't signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your computer asks before opening it.

Mac: drag Copycat to Applications and open it. When macOS says it can't verify the developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Windows: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
